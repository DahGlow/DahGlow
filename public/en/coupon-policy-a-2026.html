Dah Glow Coupon Policy

Use this guide to understand how to apply a coupon or discount code to an eligible Dah Glow order. The offer details shown with each promotion and the final amount displayed at checkout apply to your specific purchase.

How to use a coupon code

Enter your discount code in the discount field during Shopify checkout, then apply it and review the updated order total before placing your order. Checkout will show whether the code was accepted.

Check the promotion terms

Each coupon or promotion may have its own eligibility requirements. Before using a code, read the offer details for any stated restrictions, qualifying items or orders, and applicable dates.

A code may not apply to every product or order. The checkout result is the clearest way to confirm whether a discount applies to your cart.

Discounts and gift cards

Whether a coupon can be used with a gift card depends on the specific order and the options Shopify checkout makes available. Review the final order total before completing payment.

Visit Dah Glow Gift Cards for gift card information.

If your coupon does not work

  • Check that you entered the code correctly, including any letters or numbers.
  • Review the promotion details and confirm your cart meets the stated requirements.
  • Check the order summary after applying the code to see whether checkout accepted it.

If you still need help, contact the Dah Glow team and include the code and a description of the issue. Do not send payment details.

Please note: The terms supplied with a specific offer and the prices and discounts displayed in Shopify checkout govern that promotion. This page is a general guide and does not promise that a particular code will apply to an order.

Dah Glow Coupon Policy

Understand how to use Dah Glow coupons, discount codes, and promotional offers at checkout.

This policy explains the general rules for coupons and promotional discount codes issued by Dah Glow. Each offer may have its own eligibility requirements, qualifying products, minimum purchase, discount value, and validity period. The terms shown with the offer and at checkout apply to that promotion.

If an offer-specific term conflicts with this general policy, follow the terms presented with the offer, subject to applicable law. Review your order summary before placing an order to confirm the discount has been applied.

1. Coupon eligibility and offer details

Coupons are valid only for the customer, order, products, region, and dates described in the offer. Some offers may require a minimum spend or exclude selected items, sale products, shipping charges, taxes, or other fees.

Check the coupon message and checkout details for the exact value, eligible items, qualifying spend, start date, and expiration. A coupon that is expired, not yet active, already redeemed, or not valid for the items in your cart may not apply.

2. How to use a Dah Glow discount code

Enter the coupon or promo code in the discount-code field at checkout and select the option to apply it. Confirm the discount appears in your order summary before completing payment. If the code does not apply, review its requirements and make sure it was entered correctly.

Only discounts displayed in the final checkout summary are included in the order total. Contact our team before placing your order if you need help with an eligible offer.

3. Coupon types and eligible purchases

Depending on the promotion, a coupon may provide a fixed amount off, a percentage discount, or another benefit such as a shipping offer. The specific promotion states how the benefit is calculated and which products or order amounts qualify.

Unless the offer says otherwise, a coupon is a promotional discount and is not cash, store credit, or a gift card. Coupons cannot be exchanged for cash.

4. Combining coupons and other discounts

Whether a coupon can be combined with another code, automatic discount, sale price, or gift card depends on the offer terms and checkout configuration. If combinations are not permitted, checkout may accept only one eligible discount.

The order summary at checkout shows the discounts applied to your order. Please review it before submitting payment.

5. Expiration, use, and availability

Coupons must be used within the validity period stated in the offer. Unless the offer expressly allows multiple uses, a coupon is intended for one eligible redemption and cannot be reused after it has been applied to a completed order.

Promotions may be limited in quantity, time, or availability. Dah Glow may correct an obvious offer error or suspend a promotion where permitted by law; a change will not affect rights that cannot be limited under applicable law.

6. Returns, cancellations, and coupon adjustments

If an order paid with a coupon is cancelled or returned, any refund is calculated under the applicable Returns & Refunds policy and the offer terms. The final amount may reflect the discount originally applied to the eligible items.

A used coupon may not be restored after cancellation or return. If a promotion has an order threshold, removing or returning items may affect whether the remaining order still qualifies. Check the refund summary or contact customer care if you have questions.

7. Questions about coupons

For help with an active coupon or a discount shown at checkout, contact Dah Glow customer care and include the offer details. Do not send payment card information or a gift card code in a support request.
