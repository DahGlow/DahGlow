How to Pay

Choose from the payment options shown for your order at Shopify checkout.

01

Credit and debit cards

VISA
Mastercard
Maestro
American Express
Discover
Diners Club

If you choose a card option at checkout, enter the requested card details in Shopify’s secure payment form. Check the card number and expiration details before submitting. Dah Glow does not receive your full card number.

02

PayPal

PayPal

If PayPal is available for your order, select it during checkout and follow the instructions provided by PayPal. Review the final amount before authorizing payment.

03

Venmo

Venmo

When Venmo is offered at checkout, choose it and follow the prompts to authorize your payment through Venmo.

04

Afterpay

Afterpay

If Afterpay appears as an option for your order, its checkout flow will show any available payment schedule and terms for your review before you confirm.

05

Cash App Pay

Cash App

If Cash App Pay is presented at checkout, select it and complete the authorization in the Cash App payment flow.

06

Klarna

Klarna

Where Klarna is available, review its payment options, eligibility, and terms in the Klarna checkout experience before confirming your order.

07

Affirm

Affirm

If Affirm is available for your purchase, the payment plan and applicable terms will be shown in its checkout flow for you to review.

08

Zip

Zip

If Zip is offered for your order, its payment schedule and terms will be displayed before you complete the payment.

i

Before you place your order

The payment methods pictured here are for reference. Options depend on your location, order, and current checkout settings; Shopify checkout is the source of truth. Confirm the currency, discounts, taxes, and final total shown there before paying. Any installment eligibility or terms are set by the payment provider.
