Dah Glow Gift Card Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the current Dah Glow Gift Card purchase and redemption process. Review the details shown at checkout before buying, and contact us if you need help with a specific gift card.

Purchase and payment

Gift card purchases are completed through Shopify checkout. Dah Glow issues a Shopify Gift Card only after payment for the order has been independently verified. The amount, currency, and other purchase details shown at checkout apply to your order.

Gift card delivery

Shopify sends the gift card notification to the recipient email address entered during purchase. If you choose a future delivery time, Shopify handles the scheduled notification and aims to send it within an hour of the selected time after payment is verified.

Check that the recipient email address is correct before completing checkout. Delivery timing can be affected by email-provider processing or filtering.

Redeeming a gift card

Enter the gift card code in the gift card field at Shopify checkout. Shopify validates the code and applies any eligible remaining balance to the order. The checkout determines whether a gift card can be combined with a discount for that purchase; review the final total before paying.

Checking the balance

Use the secure balance checker on the Dah Glow Gift Cards page to request the current balance and status from Shopify. Keep your gift card code private and share it only with the intended recipient.

Expiration, refunds, and applicable law

Any expiration date or refund information displayed for a gift card or order, together with applicable law, governs its treatment. This page does not create an expiration period or guarantee refund eligibility. Contact Dah Glow before purchase if you need clarification about a particular gift card.

Need help? For a delivery or balance question, contact Dah Glow. Never include a full gift card code or payment credentials in a public message.
