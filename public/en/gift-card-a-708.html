Dah Glow Gift Card Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the current Dah Glow Gift Card purchase and redemption process. Review the details shown at checkout before buying, and contact us if you need help with a specific gift card.

Purchase and payment

Gift card purchases are completed through Shopify checkout. Dah Glow issues a Shopify Gift Card only after payment for the order has been independently verified. The amount, currency, and other purchase details shown at checkout apply to your order.

Gift card delivery

Shopify sends the gift card notification to the recipient email address entered during purchase. If you choose a future delivery time, Shopify handles the scheduled notification and aims to send it within an hour of the selected time after payment is verified.

Check that the recipient email address is correct before completing checkout. Delivery timing can be affected by email-provider processing or filtering.

Redeeming a gift card

Enter the gift card code in the gift card field at Shopify checkout. Shopify validates the code and applies any eligible remaining balance to the order. The checkout determines whether a gift card can be combined with a discount for that purchase; review the final total before paying.

Checking the balance

Use the secure balance checker on the Dah Glow Gift Cards page to request the current balance and status from Shopify. Keep your gift card code private and share it only with the intended recipient.

Expiration, refunds, and applicable law

Any expiration date or refund information displayed for a gift card or order, together with applicable law, governs its treatment. This page does not create an expiration period or guarantee refund eligibility. Contact Dah Glow before purchase if you need clarification about a particular gift card.

Need help? For a delivery or balance question, contact Dah Glow. Never include a full gift card code or payment credentials in a public message.

Gift Card Terms and Conditions

Read how Dah Glow digital gift cards are purchased, delivered, redeemed, and protected.

These terms apply to Dah Glow gift cards purchased through our website. Gift cards are issued after payment is verified, and digital delivery is sent to the recipient email provided with the order. The gift card email and applicable law govern where these terms differ.

1. Gift card redemption and limitations

Use a valid Dah Glow gift card toward eligible purchases at Dah Glow by entering its code in the gift card field at Shopify checkout. The available balance is applied to the order total according to checkout rules. If the balance does not cover the full amount, another accepted payment method may be needed for the remainder.

Gift card eligibility, permitted uses, and any restrictions are subject to the gift card details, the options shown at checkout, and applicable law. A gift card is not a coupon or promotional discount.

2. Purchasing a Dah Glow gift card

Gift cards may be purchased in the amounts shown on the Dah Glow gift card page. Custom amounts, if offered, must meet the minimum and maximum displayed in the purchase form. Prices are shown in the currency specified at checkout.

To complete a purchase, provide accurate buyer and recipient details and pay through the checkout options presented. A gift card is issued only after Shopify confirms the order payment. A checkout confirmation or purchase request alone does not mean a gift card has been issued.

3. Digital gift card delivery

Digital gift cards are delivered to the recipient email address entered during purchase. If scheduled delivery is available, choose a future date and time in the purchase form; delivery timing may be affected by email services and processing.

Please check the recipient email address carefully before placing the order. If the gift card email does not arrive, check spam or junk folders and then contact customer care with the order details. Do not send a complete gift card code in an email or support message.

4. Checking your gift card balance

Use the balance checker on the Dah Glow gift cards page to review the balance and status of a gift card. Enter the code only in the secure balance checker or at checkout. Keep the code private because anyone who has it may be able to use the available balance.

The balance and status shown by Shopify are the current record for the gift card. Allow for recently completed purchases or refunds to appear in the balance.

5. Validity, expiration, and refunds

Any expiration date or validity information shown with a gift card applies, subject to the consumer protection laws in the place of purchase. Where local law prohibits expiration or requires a longer validity period, applicable law takes precedence.

Gift card purchases, unused balances, and orders paid with a gift card are handled under applicable law and the relevant Dah Glow store policies. If an order paid in whole or part with a gift card is refunded, the refund method is determined by the order’s payment details and the applicable returns policy. See our Returns & Refunds policy before purchasing.

6. Gift card security and delivery issues

Keep gift card codes and related emails secure. Dah Glow cannot guarantee recovery of value after a code has been disclosed or used by someone else. Contact us promptly if a gift card email was sent to the wrong address or you believe a code has been exposed; we will review what options are available.

You are responsible for providing an accurate recipient email address. Dah Glow is not responsible for delivery problems caused by an incorrect address or email filtering, but our customer care team can help investigate.

7. Changes and customer support

We may update these terms when our gift card service or legal requirements change. Updated terms apply to future purchases; rights attached to previously issued gift cards remain subject to the terms provided at purchase and applicable law.

For questions about an order, delivery, or gift card balance, contact Dah Glow customer care. Include your order number, but never include the full gift card code or payment card details.
